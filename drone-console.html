Control Documentation

Fly with the on-screen controls or the keyboard.

  • Stick 1: up and down climbs, left and right yaws.
  • Stick 2: up and down pitches, left and right rolls.
  • When all controls are released, the estimated flight model levels out and descends at 0.5 m/s.
  • Keyboard: click the drone view first, then use W A S D, Space and Shift for climb and descend, Q and E to yaw.
  • Maze and Line are placeholders. They stop manual input until an autopilot is added.
